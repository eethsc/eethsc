### 🔬 About Me

> **"Bridging Physics, Transistors, and Autonomous AI Intelligence."**

- ⚡ **Domain Focus:** AI-driven Electronic Design Automation (EDA), Analog & Mixed-Signal IC Design, Neuro-Symbolic Agents.
- 🎯 **Core Innovation:** Closed-loop autonomous SPICE netlist synthesis and staged physical verification frameworks based on hardware knowledge graphs (HKG) and port dependency graphs (PDG).
- 🛠️ **Current Research:** Transistor-level pure physical synthesis without behavioral shortcuts, multi-agent co-evolution (`Dual-Harness²`).
- 💡 **Interests:** Mixed-signal hardware-software co-design, EDA automation, and scalable autonomous agent architectures.
